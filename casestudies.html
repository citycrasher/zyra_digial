Women's Clothing Brand · Meta Ads + Influencer Marketing

How We Helped a Women's Clothing Brand Scale From 1 ROAS to 7+ ROAS

The brand had strong products and was spending on Meta Ads but stuck around 1 ROAS. The root cause wasn't the ads — it was a trust gap. Customers were adding to cart but not purchasing because they weren't confident in the brand.

The Problem

Stuck at 1 ROAS Despite Ad Spend

The brand was spending on Meta Ads but consistently returning around 1 ROAS — just breaking even with no profitable growth.

High Cart Abandonment

Customers were adding products to cart but not completing purchases — indicating a trust issue, not a demand issue.

Generic Ad Creatives

The existing ad creatives lacked authenticity and social proof needed to build trust with potential first-time buyers.

Our Strategy

Market Research

Identified that cart abandonment was driven by a lack of trust, not a lack of interest. Customers needed social proof before purchasing.

Influencer Partnerships

Identified and partnered with the right influencers to create authentic video content that would build trust with potential customers.

Influencer Content as Meta Ads

Instead of using influencer videos only for organic reach, used the best-performing creator content as paid Meta Ad assets — combining authenticity with scale.

The Outcome

Within just 1 month, the brand went from 1 ROAS to 7.14 ROAS — generating ₹14,50,313+ in purchase value at a ₹20.66 cost per action. The strategy proved that instead of simply increasing ad spend, understanding why customers were adding products to their carts but not purchasing was the key to unlocking better performance.
